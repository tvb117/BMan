Keyboard Controls

Select an action (arrows + Enter, or click), then press the new key. Esc cancels.
If a key is already used elsewhere, the two bindings swap. Changes are saved automatically.

How to Play

Goal

Blow up bricks, collect power-ups and be the last one standing. Win the set number of rounds to take the match. When the timer runs out, sudden death starts: blocks fall from the sky in a spiral!

Controls

MoveBombAction
GamepadD-pad / stickA / YB / X / bumpers

Pause: Esc / P / Start. Action punches bombs (Power Glove) or uses your Llama's special move. Kick by walking into a bomb.

Sideline revenge

Died early? After a moment you float along the arena wall on a cloud. Move along the wall and press Bomb to throw a bomb 3 tiles into the arena. The dashed circle shows where it lands.

Arenas

  • Sunny Garden: the classic.
  • Conveyor Factory: belts carry players and bombs.
  • Warp Station: warp holes teleport you and bombs.
  • Glacier Rink: slippery ice, you keep sliding.
